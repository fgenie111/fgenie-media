Abstract

Most assistants wait to be called by name, answer one question, and forget. The Genie Cube is a study in the opposite: an object on your desk that keeps a constant, permitted intelligence in the room with you. It holds your context, turns what you say into saved work, asks before it acts, and leaves a receipt for everything it does. The same Genie follows you to your screen and your phone. This paper describes the object, the intelligence path behind it, a launch plan in five missions, and plainly what is not yet proven.
